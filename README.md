## 🚦 Lights out

I'm Madhu. I spent three years in travel operations at TripFactory and Thomas Cook India, where I lived in CRMs, booking data and pricing sheets. Now I'm doing an MSc in Business Analytics at DCU and turning that into SQL, Python and Power BI work. I care most about whether the numbers are actually right: my research so far has been about catching evaluation bugs and benchmark shortcuts before they turn into wrong conclusions.
